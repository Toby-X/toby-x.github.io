- Developed a joint latent-variable model for response accuracy and chain-of-thought length, with estimation via stochastic-approximation EM and data-driven initialization.
- Established identifiability and asymptotic posterior normality; characterized the role of ability-speed correlation in estimation precision.
- Examined prediction, calibration, and adaptive testing across four mathematical reasoning benchmarks; implemented the method and released code and processed data.
