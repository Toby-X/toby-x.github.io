- Developed a likelihood-free spectral estimator that combines binary responses and covariates through shared simplex geometry and heteroskedastic PCA.
- Established identifiability conditions and finite-sample entrywise error bounds; studied how covariate information contributes to mixed membership recovery.
- Implemented the estimator in R and studied estimation and prediction through simulations and an application to student proficiency profiles in TIMSS data.
